What do these categories mean?

OC — Open Category, no reservation. BC-A/B/C/D/E — Backward Classes, five sub-groups reserved for socially & educationally backward communities as classified by the AP BC Commission. SC — Scheduled Castes (the source rank list also publishes SC-II / SC-III columns). ST — Scheduled Tribes. OC-EWS — Economically Weaker Sections quota within Open Category, under the EWS reservation. Each category has a separate cutoff (last rank allotted) for Boys and Girls, since a share of every category's seats is reserved for women.

Why does this change which colleges appear?

Colleges with “for Women” in their name admit girls only and have no Boys cutoff at all — choosing Boys hides them completely. Choosing Girls considers every college, both co-ed and women-only, since girls are eligible at either.

What are AU / SVU / Statewide zones?

Andhra Pradesh counseling groups colleges into local-area zones: AU Zone (Andhra University area — the North Coastal & Godavari/Krishna belt) and SVU Zone (Sri Venkateswara University area — Rayalaseema & southern coastal districts) each reserve a share of seats for candidates local to that zone. Statewide covers deemed/private universities that counsel across the whole state rather than by local zone — some of these publish a separate rank list per zone for the very same branch, which is why you may see the same college twice.

What do Private / Self-Finance / University mean?

PVT — Private (unaided) college on the state counseling quota. SF — Self-Finance college, privately funded and fee-based. SS — Self-Supporting constituent college of a state university. UNIV — University college run directly by a state university. PU — Private University, a UGC-recognised private university running its own EAPCET-linked counseling, sometimes zone-wise.
